Added
-----

*   Add an opt-in ``geometries`` mode (``feedparser.parse(..., geometries=True)``
    or ``feedparser.GEOMETRIES = 1``) that collects GeoRSS Simple and GML
    geometries into ordered ``geometries`` lists on the feed and on each
    entry, with invalid geometries reported in ``geometries_diagnostics``.
    The existing ``where`` behavior is unchanged.
