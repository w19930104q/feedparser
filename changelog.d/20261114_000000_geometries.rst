Added
-----

*   Add an opt-in ``collect_geometries`` parameter to ``feedparser.parse()``
    (and a matching ``feedparser.COLLECT_GEOMETRIES`` module variable,
    disabled by default).

    When enabled, every GeoRSS Simple and GML ``Point``, ``LineString`` and
    ``Polygon`` geometry on a feed or entry is validated independently and
    appended to a new ``geometries`` list in document order, instead of the
    historical ``where`` result which only retains the last geometry of each
    message. Polygons keep their exterior ring and all interior rings.

    Geometries resolve their own ``srsName`` (with GML inheritance and
    override), recognizing EPSG:4326 and OGC CRS84 via their standard URN and
    HTTP identifiers. Output coordinates are always finite, in-range,
    two-dimensional ``(longitude, latitude)`` float tuples; no projection
    conversion is performed. Invalid geometries are rejected individually with
    a new ``feedparser.GeometryWarning`` that identifies the entry and the
    geometry index, and never affect neighboring geometries or entries.

    The ``where`` result and all default behavior are unchanged.

Fixed
-----

*   Fix a crash when a GML geometry used an HTTP-form ``srsName``
    (such as ``http://www.opengis.net/def/crs/EPSG/0/4326``).
